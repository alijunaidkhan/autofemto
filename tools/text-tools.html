What Are Text Tools?

Text tools are online utilities designed to help you manipulate, transform, and analyze text quickly and efficiently. Whether you're a writer, developer, content creator, or social media manager, text manipulation tasks happen daily—from converting case formats to counting characters for Twitter posts.

Our free text tools include a powerful case converter, word counter, character counter, and more. All processing happens in your browser—your text never leaves your device.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a case converter?

A case converter is a tool that transforms text between different letter cases like uppercase, lowercase, title case, and sentence case. It's faster and more accurate than manually retyping text.

What's the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word (e.g., "The Quick Brown Fox"). Sentence case only capitalizes the first letter of sentences (e.g., "The quick brown fox"), creating a more conversational, modern tone.

Is there a character limit for Twitter/X?

Yes, Twitter/X allows 280 characters per post. Use our character counter to check your text length before posting. Meta descriptions should be 155-160 characters for optimal SEO.

Is my text data safe?

Absolutely. All text manipulation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your data never touches our servers—100% private and secure.

Can I process large amounts of text?

Yes! There's no size limit since all processing happens in your browser. Paste entire documents—the tool handles them instantly.
